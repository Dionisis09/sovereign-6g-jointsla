επιτυχίας. Διατηρεί σημαντικό aggregate SLA penalty στα 1x, χρησιμοποιεί σχεδόν τους πόρους του SLA-aware και

ολοκληρώνεται χωρίς v3 ή νέο tuning.

1 Motivation 
Test whether cold recovery headroom and finite Gold retention can repair the previously observed hardware-
grounded QoS/resource trade-off. This is the final algorithmic experiment; no v3. 
2 Why v2 Was Introduced 
Previous seeds 1–30 showed DIRECT v1 SLA 25.48% vs baseline 15.43%, Gold 35.97% vs 22.63%, and fewer ready 
replicas before bursts. These historical results motivate design only; all tables below use unseen seeds 31–60. 
3 Prior Failure Mechanism 
The earlier exploratory factorial indicated VRAM and cold costs interacting with consolidation. It did not prove a 
unique cause. Prior preburst readiness was 1.03 vs 1.66 and recovery breaches 36.94% vs 21.60%; scale-in counts 
alone did not explain the reversal. 
4 Frozen Design 
Call v1 controller once; block selected scale-ins below causal warm requirement. Retain v1 dispatch/routing and other 
decisions. Establish a finite Gold floor through Gold-only repair if nonretiring ready+pending count is below 
requirement. Preserve cooldown, feasibility, maximum 3 replicas and budget 2. Repair precedes retained actions, at 
most one/service. No independent Silver/Bronze repair. 
Parameters: H=1, Gold multiplier 1.5, Silver=sqrt(1.5), Bronze 1; Gold floor 2, other floors 1; W=5 derived from class 
cold readiness. 
5 Recovery Aware Headroom 
For backlog q requests, observed arrival rate λ requests per second, class readiness R seconds, causal estimated serial 
cycle L seconds, deadline D seconds and slack h = max(D − L, 0), the bounded warm requirement is: 
𝑟𝑒𝑞𝑢𝑖𝑟𝑒𝑑 =  𝑚𝑖𝑛(3, 𝑚𝑎𝑥(𝑓𝑙𝑜𝑜𝑟, ⌈𝑓𝑎𝑐𝑡𝑜𝑟 ×  𝑚𝑎𝑥(𝑞𝐿
ℎ , (𝑞 +  𝜆𝑅)𝐿
𝑅 +  ℎ )⌉)) 
Empty history λ=0; impossible slack with demand or no finite feasible cycle requires 3. Cycle includes current nominal 
or past completion CPU/GPU/network/resource delay, excluding service FIFO wait. It is a fluid retention estimate, not a 
guarantee, forecast or admission decision. 
6 Gold Protection 
The Gold floor cannot be created by rejecting scale-ins from an initial one-ready placement, so a bounded Gold-only 
repair is needed. Pending cold replicas count toward planned capacity, while ready capacity is logged separately. 
Infeasible deficits remain visible; no unlimited resources or altered cold delay. 
7 Causality and Information Boundaries 
Recover completed-slot per-service observed counts by algebraically inverting unchanged EWMA telemetry. W 
contains only prior completed slots. No controller reads arrivals tape, future work factors, expected rates or burst 
masks. Physical source/worker/dispatch/decisions are inherited; burst identification is exclusively retrospective. Unit 
tests compare observed history with exports, block future-tape access, verify deterministic routing and same budget.

24 Threats to Validity 
One laptop GPU calibration, three measured vision model classes mapped to 40 synthetic services; not a real 
distributed telco deployment. Fixed 10-node topology,100-slot horizon, synthetic traffic/work 

PAGE 29
JointSLA v2 Experimental Report

variance/network/migration costs, class-average hardware times. Global v1 nominal estimator remains inherited. Cold 
readiness can exceed strict deadlines. Fluid headroom uses serial cycle estimates and retrospective latency telemetry, 
not a queue stability theorem. Five engineering seeds and one candidate do not prove universality. Held-out seeds 
control tuning leakage, not external validity. Multiple testing families are explicit; secondary/class/mechanism 
interpretations remain limited. Controller clocks/RSS are host-dependent.
